# e-dialect Open Source Commitment

e-dialect’s core public community projects will continue to have publicly
available open-source versions.

The Contributor License Agreement gives the Project Manager the rights needed
to maintain a clear rights chain and, where that chain permits, offer an
alternative license. Its purpose is to support different deployment,
compliance, and commercial needs—not to withdraw open-source rights already
provided to the community.

Alternative commercial licensing and the community open-source license exist
in parallel. A version already released under an open-source license remains
available under the rights granted by that license.

This commitment does not mean that every e-dialect-related asset must be
public. Personal data, restricted corpora, recordings, model weights, secrets,
customer-specific integrations, private infrastructure, trademarks, and other
separately licensed assets are not automatically published or licensed by this
commitment.

# e-dialect 开源承诺

e-dialect 的核心公共社区项目将持续保留公开可获得的开源版本。

贡献者许可协议使项目管理方能够维护清晰的权利链，并在权利链允许时提供替代许可；
其目的是支持不同的部署、合规和商业需求，而不是撤销已经向社区提供的开源权利。

替代商业许可与社区开源许可并行存在。已经按开源许可证发布的版本，继续享有该许可
证已经授予的权利。

本承诺不表示所有 e-dialect 相关资产都必须公开。个人数据、受限语料、录音、模型
权重、密钥、客户专有集成、私有基础设施、商标及其他单独授权资产，不因本承诺自动
公开或获得软件许可证。
